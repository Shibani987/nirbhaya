About Nirvaya

Empowering Women Through Safety & Technology

Our Story

Nirvaya SOS was founded in 2025 with a single mission: to create a safer world for women through innovative technology solutions. Born out of the urgent need to address women's safety concerns in our society, Nirvaya provides immediate emergency response systems that empower women to seek help when they need it most.

The name "Nirvaya" represents fearlessness and courage - qualities we aim to instill in every woman by providing them with reliable safety tools at their fingertips. We believe that technology, when designed thoughtfully, can be a powerful ally in creating social change.

Our team consists of dedicated professionals from diverse backgrounds including technology, social work, and security, all united by a common goal of making a meaningful difference in women's lives.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and immediate safety solutions that empower women to navigate the world with confidence and security. We strive to leverage technology to create practical tools that can make a real difference in emergency situations.

Vision

A world where every woman feels safe, secure, and empowered to pursue her dreams without fear. We envision a society where technology serves as an enabler of equality and protection, contributing to a fundamental shift in women's safety.

The Team Behind Nirvaya

Our diverse team brings together expertise in technology, personal safety, gender studies, and community outreach. We are a passionate group committed to creating innovative solutions that address real-world problems affecting women's safety and empowerment.
