How Nirvaya SOS Works

1

Setup Your Profile

Register on the Nirvaya app and create your profile with essential information. Add up to 5 emergency contacts who will be notified during emergencies. These trusted individuals will receive alerts with your real-time location when you activate the SOS feature.

2

Carry Your Nirvaya Device

The compact Nirvaya device can be easily attached to your clothing, bag, or worn as an accessory. The device maintains a constant Bluetooth connection with your smartphone and features a dedicated SOS button that can be quickly accessed in emergency situations.

3

Activate in Emergencies

In an emergency situation, you can activate the SOS feature using one of three methods:
- Press the SOS button on the physical device
- Use the SOS button in the mobile application
- Use voice commands like "Nirvaya Help" or "Nirvaya Emergency" (device must be within hearing range)

4

Automatic Alert System

Once activated, Nirvaya immediately sends alerts to all your emergency contacts with your precise GPS location, which updates in real-time as you move. The system also triggers a loud alarm to deter attackers and alert people nearby that you need help.

5

Emergency Response

Your emergency contacts receive SMS alerts, app notifications, and automated calls informing them of your distress situation. They can view your real-time location through a secure link and coordinate assistance. The premium version includes direct connection to local emergency services where available.
