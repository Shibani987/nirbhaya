WOMEN SAFETY TIPS

Share Your Location

Always let someone trusted know your whereabouts when traveling alone. Use location-sharing apps to keep friends and family informed of your movements.

Keep Phone Charged

Ensure your phone is always charged before heading out. Carry a portable power bank for emergencies when you might need to call for help.

Stay Aware

Be mindful of your surroundings. Avoid using headphones or being distracted by your phone in isolated areas or late at night.

Self-Defense Basics

Learn basic self-defense techniques. Awareness and preparedness are key to personal safety in unexpected situations.

Safe Transportation

Choose verified transportation services. Check driver details and share ride information with trusted contacts before getting in.

Use Safety Apps

Install safety apps like Nirvaya that can quickly alert emergency contacts with your location with just a tap or voice command.
